Investment • Equity

Stocks

Stocks represent ownership in a company and can provide returns through price appreciation and dividends.

Snapshot

Risk: Medium–High

Return: Variable

Liquidity: High for actively traded stocks

Time horizon: Short to Long Term

What it is & how it works

When you buy a stock, you own a small portion of a company. The stock price changes according to supply, demand, company performance, economic conditions and investor expectations. You can potentially earn through price appreciation and, for some companies, dividends.

Example

If you buy a stock at ₹100 and later sell it at ₹120, your price gain is ₹20 per share before costs and taxes.

Potential rewards

  • Potential for capital growth
  • Possible dividend income
  • High liquidity in actively traded stocks

Key risks

  • Prices can fall significantly
  • Company-specific risk
  • Market volatility

Best suited for

Investors who can accept market fluctuations and want exposure to company growth.
